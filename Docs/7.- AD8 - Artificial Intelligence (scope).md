# EPSO/AD/430/26 — Field 1 – Artificial intelligence (AI)

Official scope of the field: Annex II (“Typical duties”) of the competition
notice, copied verbatim. This is the ground the field-related multiple-choice
test is drawn from — 30 questions, 40 minutes, pass mark 15/30, in Language 2.

> Source: Official Journal C/2026/4668 of 8.9.2026 —
> https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/4668/oj
> **The notice text prevails over anything summarised in this platform.**

The duties outlined below, grouped into categories 1 to 3, largely reflect those typically associated with roles related to AI in both private and public contexts, including in research and innovation. The performance of any of the categories of duties listed requires technical expertise. The successful candidates may be assigned any of the duties listed below, either individually or in combination, depending on the needs of the service. Similar or related duties may be assigned if required by the needs of the service.


### 1. Design, building, validation, and operation of end-to-end AI solutions (technical specialisation in AI)

- Providing advice and contribute to decision-making, in particular on:
- AI technologies, architectures, algorithms, and design patterns suitable for enterprise-scale application;
    - build/buy choices;
    - model/algorithm selection and optimisation.
- Leading AI projects and/or AI teams that develop, deploy, scale, and operate production-ready AI solutions with business impact.
- Ensuring the foundations for AI at scale, such as:
- system security;
    - regulatory compliance;
    - full Machine Learning Operations (MLOps);
    - product lifecycle (monitoring, maintenance, continuous improvement);
- testing and quality assurance activities.
- Ensuring data readiness to support AI training and AI systems operations, including:
- data preparation;
    - pipelines;
    - quality;
    - security and robustness;
- governance.
- Providing advanced (level 2 / level 3) technical support and guidance to cross-functional teams.
- Ensuring collaboration and communication with cross-functional teams such as data scientists, software engineers, and business analysts to align AI solutions with organisational goals.
- Operating AI solutions and providing guidance to operators and users to ensure that the AI solutions are used effectively.
- Technology watch in areas such as:
- generative AI model;
    - Application Programming Interfaces (APIs);
    - cloud technologies;
    - AI interoperability and integration technologies;
- Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG);
    - protocols such as model context protocol (MCP);
    - agentic AI.

### 2. Ensuring compliant, defensible, and scalable AI use (specialisation in AI governance, compliance, and enablement)

- Providing advice on AI governance frameworks, legal requirements, and risk management to ensure compliant and defensible AI use.
- Ensuring compliance with EU regulations related to AI, in particular the Artificial Intelligence Act (1), corporate policies on AI, data protection, and security. For example, designing tools, methodologies, and benchmarks for evaluating model capabilities and risks.
- Overseeing design, evaluation, and testing of AI systems to meet organisational standards for trustworthiness, reliability, auditability, fairness, and safety, both before deployment and during use.
- Safeguarding explainability, traceability, and reproducibility of outputs used as evidence or in decision-making.
- Ensuring collaboration and communication with technical, policy, and procurement teams to embed compliance and ethical requirements into AI projects and contracts.
- Contributing to AI literacy, including defining requirements and supervision of their implementation.

### 3. Policymaking and policy enforcement in the field of AI (policy specialisation in AI)

- Providing advice on the interplay between AI technologies and policy domains, including AI technology design and deployment, industrial applications, product integration, etc.
- Providing advice on AI technologies, overall emerging trends and societal impact to support policy and enforcement objectives.
- Technology watch on AI development and trends. For example, monitoring the general-purpose AI ecosystem, technological and market developments, and the emergence of new risks.
- Assessing AI strategies of private and public entities with respect to their relevance for policymaking and policy enforcement objectives.
- Ensuring that policymaking and policy-enforcement decisions touching on AI technologies are technically sound.
- Representing the institution/entity as an expert towards external stakeholders.
- Providing guidance, sharing awareness, delivering training sessions, and offering advice on AI-related aspects.
